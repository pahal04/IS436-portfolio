Hello!

I'm Pahal, an Information Systems student at UMBC who loves the intersection of technology and people. I'm drawn to problems that matter: how do systems fail, how do they scale, and what makes a product actually feel good to use?

This semester in IS 436, I've been through the full software development lifecycle, from writing requirements documents and system diagrams to building and deploying a real full-stack application with Docker. It's been equal parts challenging and exciting.

Outside of class, I help organize hackUMBC. Putting together spaces where students can build something meaningful in 24 hours. I believe the best ideas come from people who care, and hackathons are great proof of that. I'm also an incoming Program Manager Intern at Amazon this summer, where I'll be working hands on with a team to make real impact on a product used by millions.
